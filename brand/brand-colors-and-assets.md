# Writespace brand tokens

**Brand line:** For ideas that deserve more than a scroll.

| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight navy | `#191638` | Primary mark, headings, primary buttons, dark illustrations |
| Coral | `#FF765E` | Wordmark accent, key highlights, small emphasis |
| Lavender | `#897AF0` | Secondary accent and small decorative details |
| Warm ivory | `#F8F6F1` | Email canvas and quiet editorial backgrounds |
| Warm grey | `#928C86` | Secondary wordmark segment |
| Body text | `#4E4A61` | Main readable copy |

The PDF reference has a mostly white editorial layout, a refined uppercase serif wordmark, coral emphasis, and dark navy illustrations with restrained lavender details. Email backgrounds therefore remain very light; navy and coral provide contrast without turning the emails into dark promotional banners.

## Logo files

- `writespace-logo.svg` / `.png`: primary horizontal lockup with tagline.
- `writespace-email-logo.svg` / `.png`: email-safe horizontal lockup without the tagline; the email layout renders the tagline as live text.
- `writespace-mark.svg` / `.png`: standalone square brand mark.
- `writespace-app-icon.svg` / `.png`: app-icon canvas.
- `writespace-logo-on-dark.svg` / `.png`: light wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `writespace-logo-monochrome.svg` / `.png`: single-colour wordmark.

SVG files are the master artwork. PNG files are raster exports for contexts such as email clients that do not reliably render SVG.
